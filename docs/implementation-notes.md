# Streamlined implementation notes (rules v10)

The complete rule sheet is [public/rules.md](../public/rules.md). Basic and Riichi retain their original rules. The notes below apply to the separate Streamlined mode:

- The starting dealer is seeded randomly. A game ends after the selected 1–4 dealer orbits (one by default), including crossing eliminated seats. Chips, loans, and sticks carry across these orbits; tournament resets occur only between games. Basic players unable to ante are eliminated; Expansion players automatically borrow 200 only when needed for an ante, at most once per game. A later unaffordable ante eliminates them while retaining their remaining chips and 250-point loan deduction. The allowance carries across orbits and resets between tournament games; loans cannot be repaid.
- Tournament games reset chips to 200/300/400/500, antes to 5/10/15/20, and reset loans and elimination; carry unused fishing sticks forward. Streamlined starts each player with three Riichi sticks and adds three at the start of each subsequent game, with no grants between rounds. Adjusted scores accumulate across games.
- Each lane starts with one card. Basic skips Charleston; Expansion passes two cards left simultaneously. Reveals are simultaneous 2/2 between three betting streets; hands contain six cards and score at most four. Long Chow is four consecutive cards.
- Check is available only at no cost. Check and Call each include one free fish in both modes; Call pays only the difference. Bet/Raise gives no free fish. Expansion permits one stick per turn for a second fish after Check/Call or one fish after Bet. All-in Calls retain normal fishing and optional stick use, even when the caller spends their last chips; an all-in Bet gives no fishing.
- The dealer opens Street 1. Later streets start with the last bettor from the preceding street, or the dealer if nobody bet, skipping folded players.
- All-in caps only the current street. Excess street contributions are refunded, previous-street money remains in the pot, and further bets and raises stop. A shorter subsequent call lowers the cap again. Remaining players call or fold, finish their normal fishing and optional stick decisions, then proceed directly to showdown; there are no side pots. Spending the final chips on the ante also triggers showdown after setup.
- A Blank exchanges with any depth of either lane, leaving the Blank at that exact position, and replaces one fishing action without a separate discard.
- There is no Riichi declaration, hand lock, or winner stick award. Unused fishing sticks never expire during a tournament. One stick per turn remains the limit, including a fish before betting.
- Basic has nine ranks and Expansion ten, including the four-card Long Chow. Ties compare defining combinations before pairs, descending; Winds outrank Dragons, which outrank numbered cards. Unused kickers do not count. Exact ties split exactly, including fractional chips. A fractional remaining stack can be bet all-in.
- Public views never expose folded or uncontested concealed cards through result history. Invalid actions leave chips, cards, events, and randomness unchanged.

Reserved zero/empty Curse and Lotus result fields remain for report consumers, but Streamlined has no Lotus cards, disqualification, or bonus. Legacy curse action fields are retained only for explicit rejection. Rules v9 and older saves are rejected to avoid mixing stick economies and hand rules. Optional loans below 100 chips are available during Charleston; loans cannot be repaid.
