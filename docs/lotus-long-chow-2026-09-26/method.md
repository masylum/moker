# Lotus scaling and Long Chow: paired experiment

Four arms: current rules, scaled Lotus payout only, Long Chow only, and both. Each arm contains 60 matched four-game tournaments (one dealer orbit per constituent game), four heuristic players, 24 equity samples and ten process workers. Chips reset to 200/300/400/500; antes to 5/10/15/20. Starting sticks remain three per player per game. These are 240 analyzed tournaments / 960 constituent games, not 960 independent tournament observations.

Control Lotus reward is 3 antes per opponent: 15/30/45/60 chips. The experimental reward is (game number + 2) antes: 15/40/75/120. Payments remain capped at the payer's chips and do not trigger a loan. Engine payouts, bot betting reward estimates and secured-lead liability use the same multiplier. Game 1 is identical between each matched standard/scaled reward pair and is checked exactly.

Long Chow is five consecutive numbers of one suit. Matching-color Jokers may fill a gap. It ranks 10, between Three Dragons (9) and Three Dragons + Eyes (11). Four Winds becomes 12, Kong 13, and the bot's Twin Lotus sentinel becomes 14. Basic scoring excludes Long Chow. Both candidate generation and bot progress matching include the new hand. Production game rules are unchanged: variants are applied to isolated source snapshots.

Samples use seed prefix `lotus-long-20260926`. Manifests contain the rank order, rule flags and source hashes. `handDiagnostics` records played holdings; `lotusObservations` records pre-decision Lotus ownership and the chosen action. A bet while holding one Lotus can also fish it away or complete Twin Lotus, so these bets are reported separately from bets without fishing. Successful Lotus bluff hands are measured from actual payouts. Retained combinations include folded holdings, while showdown category counts exclude folded and Lotus-disqualified holdings. These measures have different denominators.

Uncertainty uses 4,000 matched whole-tournament bootstrap resamples. Rare/zero Lotus payouts cannot be assigned strong population conclusions from degenerate bootstrap intervals. Fixed bot strategy remains a limitation, including its fixed single-Lotus hand-building penalty and limited multi-street planning.

The initial scaled-only batch updated payout and betting utility but missed a separate secured-lead liability expression. It was superseded and rerun with the same seeds after correcting that expression. Its files are retained under `superseded/` and excluded from the analysis. Total execution: 300 tournaments; unique analyzed sample: 240. The combined batch includes the correction from the start.

Reproduce with `npx tsx scripts/ladder-experiment.ts --mode riichi --order high-card,eye,chow,two-eyes,chow-eye,pung,three-winds,pung-eye,three-dragons,three-dragons-eye,four-winds,kong --count 60 --workers 10 --orbits 1 --games 4 --starting-sticks 3 --seed lotus-long-20260926 --out docs/lotus-long-chow-2026-09-26/control.jsonl`. Add `--scaled-lotus` for the reward variant. Add `--long-chow` and insert `long-chow` after `three-dragons` in the order for the Long Chow variant. Use both flags for the combined arm, and distinct output files for all arms. Run `python3 scripts/analyze-lotus-long-chow.py` after all four files are complete.
